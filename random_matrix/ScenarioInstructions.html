Imagine you are looking for a rental home in Sweden. On the next pages you will see six pairs of homes. For each pair, please choose the home you would rent.

Both homes in a pair are near the same type of power plant, either a nuclear power plant or an oil-fired power plant. The type may differ between pairs. In all cases, the plant is in regular operation.

The two homes in each pair can differ in two ways:

  • Monthly rent
  • Distance to the power plant

The homes are otherwise identical, including their size, condition, neighbourhood amenities, and travel time to work or study. Moving costs are the same. The plant is not visible from either home.

The standard rent for these homes is SEK 6,500 per month. Some homes cost more: for example, a home with a rent of SEK 7,500 costs SEK 1,000 more per month, for as long as you live there.

Please consider your household budget: paying more rent would leave less money for other purchases or savings.

Each pair is a separate choice. Please answer each one as if it were the only choice you had.
